# Gate P3 — Daemon, MCP and certified read

Status: **G0 passed (2026-10-20); implementation in progress (milestone 1).** [ADR 0008](../adr/0008-p3-daemon-sessions-certificates.md) was accepted at revision 3.2 after four independent review rounds. Round 1 found 5 blockers, 14 majors and 12 minors; round 2 found 1 blocker, 7 majors and 9 minors; round 3 found 4 majors and 6 minors; the confirmation found 1 major. All are closed. SPEC v1.5.0–v1.5.3 records the amendments. The P2 gate passed (see [P2.md](P2.md)).

## 0. Scope

- SPEC §5 P3, §6.3 (MCP tools), §6.4 (Certificate); SD13, SD14(b), SD15, SD16, SD18.3; I4, I6.
- Owner decisions (2026-10-20):
  - sessions, read ledgers and certificates live in their own append-only session log;
  - MCP is hand-rolled JSON-RPC over stdio.
- Milestones are listed in ADR 0008 §12.
- Explicitly deferred:
  - hooks and action gating (P4);
  - path-filtered checks (P5a);
  - branches, WHAT_IF, `ctx_commit` (P6);
  - conflicts and validators (P7);
  - hybrid retrieval and packing (P8);
  - multiple principals and ACL (P9).
