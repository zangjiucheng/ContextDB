# Gate P3 — Daemon, MCP and certified read

Status: **Passed (2026-10-21).** G0 passed on 2026-10-20. Independent review findings, the closure check N1–N3, and the closure confirmation's two minors are all closed (ADR 0008 §18). [ADR 0008](../adr/0008-p3-daemon-sessions-certificates.md) was accepted at revision 3.2 after four independent review rounds. Round 1 found 5 blockers, 14 majors and 12 minors; round 2 found 1 blocker, 7 majors and 9 minors; round 3 found 4 majors and 6 minors; the confirmation found 1 major. All are closed. SPEC v1.5.0–v1.5.3 records the amendments. The P2 gate passed (see [P2.md](P2.md)).
