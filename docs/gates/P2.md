# Gate P2 — Durability and recovery

Status: **local gate layers pass.** The independent review found 1 blocker, 5 major and 6 minor findings; all are fixed with regression tests, and the reviewer's closure check confirmed all 12 closed. Its 1 major and 5 minor new findings (N1–N6) are fixed. Still pending: the reviewer's confirmation of N1–N6, remote CI and the nightly run.
See [P2-report.md](P2-report.md), [durable-log ADR 0006](../adr/0006-p2-durable-log.md) and
[incremental structural commits ADR 0007](../adr/0007-incremental-structural-commits.md).

## 0. Scope

- SPEC §5 P2, §6.1 (read modes, input contract 1), §6.2 batch-level checks, §7 (op_log fields).
- Involved SDs: SD7 (inputs), SD8 (statuses, history), I5 (durable prefix).
- `contextdb-store`:
  - op_log as an own append-only segment log (the source of truth) behind the `Durability` trait;
  - content-addressed blobs;
  - snapshots and replay;
  - SQLite projections (`diff_log`, `head_history`, `base_history`) with a watermark;
  - HISTORICAL / SNAPSHOT / DIFF over persisted data;
  - format version 1 and the migration framework;
  - the crash-injection harness.
- `ctx replay | doctor [--rebuild | --truncate-before E [--yes]] | snapshot | migrate | export`.
- Core: incremental structural commits (ADR 0007), so cold-start replay meets G5.
- Explicitly deferred:
  - the daemon/MCP and certificates (P3);
  - erasure and shredding (P9; P2 never deletes a blob);
  - a signed head (P9 threat model, ADR 0006 §2).
