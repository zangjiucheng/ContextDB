### G4

- **`ctxd-validate-stress 1800`** on the remote Linux host: 4 socket writers (2 on `main`, 2 on `dev`) and 4 workers with command validators (trusted on `main`, untrusted on `dev`). Result: 99,253 premise moves, 10,869 records and 16,490 worker reviews, 27,359 jobs done and 135,688 superseded, 33,120 validator calls and **0 violations**. The invariants checked:
  - no duplicate record or review per `(branch, rule, curvec)`;
  - no record from the LLM principal;
  - no Pending rule left on `main`, and every Pending rule on `dev` has a review naming its vector;
  - no job left queued, running or failed.

  This run predates the batched discovery, so it is rerun with G7.
- **Process death at each job step** (`p7_g4`, 25 runs per step and kind): a real `ctxd` child aborts at `claimed`, `called`, `committed` or `done` (`CTX_JOB_CRASH`, debug builds). Each restart ends with exactly one record (trusted) or one PendingReview (LLM). The validator is called twice only after `called`, and once otherwise.
- **Disk full** (`p7_g4`):
  - The blob store fails the worker's commit at `append`, `sync` and `write`. The store poisons; the job is requeued without using an attempt; after a restart it records once, with at most one repeated call.
  - The job table cannot grow (`limit_pages`). Discovery fails without losing its place; claims and finishes fail and are retried after restart. All 400 rules end with exactly one record, with no Failed row.

### G6

Real `claude -p` (claude 2.1.317, `claude-haiku-5-5`, the sandboxed `ClaudeCli`, 4 workers), 2026-11-03. The items were built by `p7_g6 collect`:

- 125 rule tasks: 5 real premise changes from the three dogfood stores, plus 120 generated (20 domains × 3 conclusions × {moved, re-confirmed}). The dogfood stores cite with Cur and Pin only, so no real Sem rule exists.
- 128 candidate pairs: the 88 real candidates in the dogfood stores, plus 40 generated (20 domains × {contradicting, compatible}).

Generated items are labelled by construction. The dogfood items were labelled by two independent agents that never saw the LLM output; an item where they differ is disputed. Raw items and outcomes contain project text and stay out of the repository; [`p7-g6-score.txt`](p7-g6-score.txt) has the scores by item id.

- **Calls.** 253 ok, 0 errors; 384,214 tokens in and 90,200 out; latency p50 3.2 s, p95 6.0 s.
- **Agreement:**
  - generated pairs 40/40;
  - dogfood pairs 86/88: two `none` pairs judged `conflict`, which become non-gating PendingReviews;
  - generated rules 114/120: four `moved` cases labelled `new_rule` instead of `conflict`, both of which give a review; two `re-confirmed` cases labelled `none` instead of `reinforcement`;
  - dogfood rules 3/4 (1 disputed, the other mislabelled `conflict` for `boundary_change`).

  Kernel correctness is separate: no LLM outcome can write a record (T50).
- **O2.** The dogfood stores have no Sem rule, so 0 jobs over 173 head-moving epochs. The generator (1,000 `generate_p7` seeds) gives 2,131 jobs over 3,513 head-moving epochs (mean 0.61, max 2 per move). A per-revision union vector would save 0 calls (no two Pending rules share a conclusion revision). No case for union vectors yet; validation stays per rule.
- **`candidate_min_score`.** Every one of the 20 reference conflicts is a generated pair; none of the 88 real candidates was labelled a conflict. Every threshold up to 5.0 keeps all 20 conflicts. At 5.0, 32 of the 108 non-conflicts remain (at 1.0, all 108). The generated scores come from a 60-record index, so they do not transfer to real stores. The default stays at 1.0, and the calibration is redone once real conflicts exist.

## 3. Independent implementation review (2026-11-03)

A reviewer who did not implement P7 read commits `69a9522..cf556e0` against ADR 0015 and SPEC SD10/SD17/SD20 and §6.1 contract 14, and ran scratch tests outside the repository. **No blocker.** It found these invariants intact:
- an LLM never records;
- exactly once per key;
- an admin verdict is never overwritten;
- handoff is accepted at most once;
- candidates are visible on the branch;
- `ctx_changes` pages are correct;
- the `claude -p` sandbox holds.

| # | Severity | Finding | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | major (confirmed by a scratch test) | `claim` read only the 256 oldest claimable rows. Rows no validator's filter takes stay Queued, so 256 of them (another branch, kind or scope) blocked every later job. | **Fixed:** `claim` pages through the rows by rowid. Test `declined_rows_never_hide_a_claimable_one` (600 declined rows). |
| m1 | minor | Pair judging used an attempt on a store error, so pairs were left `Failed` after recovery. | **Fixed:** `pair_defer` releases the pair without an attempt (counted in `store_errors`), as `defer` does for rule jobs. |
