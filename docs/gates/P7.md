### G4

- **`ctxd-validate-stress 1800`** on the remote Linux host: 4 socket writers (2 on `main`, 2 on `dev`) and 4 workers with command validators (trusted on `main`, untrusted on `dev`). The invariants checked:
  - no duplicate record or review per `(branch, rule, curvec)`;
  - no record from the LLM principal;
  - no Pending rule left on `main`, and every Pending rule on `dev` has a review naming its vector;
  - no job left queued, running or failed.

  Results:
  - **Rerun** (2026-11-03, with batched discovery; [`p7-g4-stress.txt`](p7-g4-stress.txt)): 96,576 premise moves, 11,614 records and 17,495 worker reviews, 29,109 jobs done and 136,590 superseded, 35,085 validator calls, 0 store errors and **0 violations**.
  - First run: 99,253 moves, 0 violations.
- **Process death at each job step** (`p7_g4`, 25 runs per step and kind): a real `ctxd` child aborts at `claimed`, `called`, `committed` or `done` (`CTX_JOB_CRASH`, debug builds). Each restart ends with exactly one record (trusted) or one PendingReview (LLM). The validator is called twice only after `called`, and once otherwise.
- **Disk full** (`p7_g4`):
  - The blob store fails the worker's commit at `append`, `sync` and `write`. The store poisons; the job is requeued without using an attempt; after a restart it records once, with at most one repeated call.
  - The job table cannot grow (`limit_pages`). Discovery fails without losing its place; claims and finishes fail and are retried after restart. All 400 rules end with exactly one record, with no Failed row.

**Commit budget (owner decision D4).** ADR 0015 asked for busy p99 within 5% of idle, with the measured writer itself moving premises of 128 rules every 5 ms. That bound could not be measured. Idle p99 alone moved from 27 to 58 ms between rounds, from per-commit `F_FULLFSYNC` jitter, and that setup gave 1.88×.

The owner restated the budget:
- **Measured commits:** ordinary records, outside every rule.
- **Background load:** a second connection moves Sem premises twice a second, at 2 jobs per move. G6's generator averages 0.61 jobs per move.
- **Rounds:** 5 interleaved idle/busy rounds, pooled.
- **Budget:** p50 ≤ 1.10×, p99 ≤ 1.25×.

Reported, not gated: at 16 jobs per move (about 50 worker record commits a second), p50 is 1.06× and p99 1.56×. Each validator record is its own durable commit through the shared group-commit writer.

  - generated rules 114/120: four `moved` cases labelled `new_rule` instead of `conflict`, both of which give a review; two `re-confirmed` cases labelled `none` instead of `reinforcement`;

  Kernel correctness is separate: no LLM outcome can write a record (T50).

## 3. Independent implementation review (2026-11-03)

A reviewer who did not implement P7 read commits `69a9522..cf556e0` against ADR 0015 and SPEC SD10/SD17/SD20 and §6.1 contract 14, and ran scratch tests outside the repository. **No blocker.** It found these invariants intact:
- an LLM never records;
- exactly once per key;
- an admin verdict is never overwritten;
- handoff is accepted at most once;
- candidates are visible on the branch;
- `ctx_changes` pages are correct;
- the `claude -p` sandbox holds.

| # | Severity | Finding | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | major (confirmed by a scratch test) | `claim` read only the 256 oldest claimable rows. Rows no validator's filter takes stay Queued, so 256 of them (another branch, kind or scope) blocked every later job. | **Fixed:** `claim` pages through the rows by rowid. Test `declined_rows_never_hide_a_claimable_one` (600 declined rows). |
| m1 | minor | Pair judging used an attempt on a store error, so pairs were left `Failed` after recovery. | **Fixed:** `pair_defer` releases the pair without an attempt (counted in `store_errors`), as `defer` does for rule jobs. |

| m3 | minor | An LLM permit was spent before the `TooLarge` check. | **Fixed:** the size check comes first, both for rule jobs and pairs. |
